# TREATMENT — SWISS MOTION · 10 s test

**Brief.** One style test, not a client film: prove that a DeepSeek-driven agent can follow
`styles/swiss-motion/STYLE.md` (lemo-opuscar, MIT) and produce a real film through the
code-render pipeline. 1920×1080, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 10.0 s, no narration (the type is the voice).

**Benchmark (grammar only).** Müller-Brockmann / Hofmann posters: modular grid, flush-left type,
extreme scale contrast, one signal colour. What not to take: any specific historical poster's
composition, Helvetica/Akzidenz files, real institution names.

**Three candidate structures**
1. *A programme* — three rules applied one by one to one page, then the system generates 66 variants. (chosen)
2. *A timetable* — 24 rows fill one beat each; too many words for 10 s.
3. *A comparison* — two systems side by side until one element crosses the gutter; needs 20 s to read.

**Logline.** A page learns three rules, keeps one exception, and becomes a machine of 66 styles.

**Arc.** setup (grid) → three rules snap on beats → the exception leaves the grid (the only red
thing, the only linear motion, the only sound in the silence) → the system scales it to 66 →
the exception is absorbed as the 66th tile → the system closes and leaves an empty grid.

**Shot list (one locked spread, one camera move)**

| t | shot | camera | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | paper wipe, dot, 12 columns grow | locked | the page is the stage; the system is born from one point |
| 1.5–2.0 | giant "3" climbs in six bands | locked | chapter number as a module |
| 2.0–3.5 | rules 01/02/03 snap; beat counter fills one 8th per hit | locked | each rule = one percussion hit |
| 3.5–6.5 | exception leaves the grid | push in 5 %, return on beat | the only camera move, to make the exception an event |
| 5.25 | knife cut through the "3" | locked | deletion is a knife cut |
| 5.5–6.3 | "66" climbs in bands | locked | same module, different quantity |
| 6.25–6.9 | wall of 66 tiles, last one red | locked | the system generates its variants |
| 6.8–9.4 | end line flush left | locked | the conclusion is one word |
| 9.4–10.0 | final cut, everything leaves upward, empty grid | locked | the system closes over the space it left |

**Beat sheet.** 120 BPM, 0.5 s beat, 0.25 s 8th. Every visual onset is on a whole frame and on the
8th grid; the exception alone is unsnapped (linear).

**Cue map.** Kick 4-on-the-floor from 0.5, hats on 8ths, motorik bass + marimba pattern from 2.0;
band thins at 4.5; **hard silence 5.0–5.5** (the exception's sine glide is the only sound);
drums return 5.5 with glockenspiel; everything stops at 9.5 except a felt thump at 9.78.

**Sound design.** Print materials: paper slap, ruling pen, letterpress clack, block snaps,
guillotine snick, tile ticks, felt thump. No reverb, dry and close.

**Type.** Inter (OFL) for Latin and figures, Noto Sans SC (OFL) for Chinese, both subset to the
characters used and self-hosted. One family, several weights; flush left, ragged right.
